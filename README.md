# EX9: Analyzing Sales Trends with PivotTables and Charts

## Topic
**Analyzing Sales Trends with PivotTables and Charts using the DMart Retail Business Dataset**

---

## Objective

The objective of this experiment is to analyze a large retail sales dataset using PivotTables and Pivot Charts in Microsoft Excel/WPS Office. The analysis helps summarize sales performance by region, product category, customer type, city, and month, enabling better understanding of business trends and decision-making.

---

## Dataset Information

**Dataset:** DMart Retail Business Dataset

The dataset contains approximately **200,000 retail transaction records** with fields such as:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Region
- City
- Category
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit
- Customer Type
- Payment Mode
- Delivery Date

---

# Task 1: Creating PivotTables

### 1. Region-wise Sales Analysis

A PivotTable was created to calculate the **total sales for each region**.

**Rows:** Region

**Values:** Sum of Sales

**Purpose:** Compare revenue generated across different geographical regions.

### 2. Category-wise Sales Analysis

A PivotTable summarizes the **total sales for each product category**.

**Rows:** Category

**Values:** Sum of Sales

**Purpose:** Identify which product categories contribute the highest revenue.

### 3. Monthly Sales Trend

The Order Date field was grouped by **Month** to analyze monthly sales performance.

**Rows:** Month

**Values:** Sum of Sales

**Purpose:** Observe seasonal sales patterns and monthly business growth.

### 4. Customer Type Distribution

A PivotTable calculates total sales made by different customer types.

**Rows:** Customer Type

**Values:** Sum of Sales

**Purpose:** Compare purchasing contribution of Member, Regular, and New customers.

### 5. Top Profitable Cities

A PivotTable identifies cities generating the highest profit.

**Rows:** City

**Values:** Sum of Profit

**Filter:** Top 5 by Profit

**Purpose:** Highlight the best-performing cities.

---

# Task 2: Creating Pivot Charts

The summarized PivotTables were converted into Pivot Charts for better visualization.

### Column Chart — Sales by Region

- X-Axis: Region
- Y-Axis: Total Sales

**Insight:** Helps compare regional revenue at a glance.

### Pie Chart — Customer Type Distribution

- Categories: Member, New, Regular
- Values: Sum of Sales

**Insight:** Shows the percentage contribution of each customer type.

### Line Chart — Monthly Sales Trend

- X-Axis: Month
- Y-Axis: Total Sales

**Insight:** Displays sales growth and seasonal fluctuations over time.

### Column Chart — Quantity Sold by Category

- X-Axis: Category
- Y-Axis: Sum of Quantity

**Insight:** Identifies the most frequently sold product categories.

### Bar Chart — Top 5 Profitable Cities

- Y-Axis: City
- X-Axis: Sum of Profit

**Insight:** Compares profit generated by the five highest-performing cities.

---

# Business Insights

- Regional analysis revealed variations in revenue across different markets.
- Category-wise comparison helped identify the strongest product segments.
- Monthly trend visualization highlighted changes in customer purchasing behavior throughout the year.
- Customer type analysis showed the sales contribution of Member, Regular, and New customers.
- Profit analysis identified the top five cities generating the highest business profit.
- Pivot Charts transformed large transactional data into clear and interactive visual reports.
